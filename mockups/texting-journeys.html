Where Better Date switches between text and screens

  • Text: learning preferences
  • Text: booking reminders and day-of reminder
  • Text: "how'd it go?" and date two
  • Text: invite to make it official
  • Screen: landing page and quiz (for search traffic)
  • Screen: the plan with map and starters
  • Screen: calendar, payment, sign-up

Free plan: one plan a week, nothing saved after the date. Pro: unlimited plans, and the AI remembers past dates ("last time you did sushi, try something new").
